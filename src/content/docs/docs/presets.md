---

param8 has **8 presets** : 6 user-configurable presets and 2 built-in modes.

## Switching Presets

Hold **Shift** to see preset names on the displays, then press any of the 8 buttons:

| Button | Preset |
|--------|--------|
| 1–6 | [User presets](/docs/user-presets) |
| 7 |  [Global mode](/docs/mixer-mode) |
| 8 | [Device mode](/docs/device-mode) |
